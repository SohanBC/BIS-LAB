PROBLEM STATEMENT:  
Students' academic performance depends on factors such as attendance, study hours, assignment marks, and internal examination marks. Traditional evaluation methods may not effectively identify the optimal combination of these factors. Therefore, Grey Wolf Optimizer (GWO) is used to optimize the weights assigned to these factors to maximize the predicted student performance score and identify students who may be at academic risk.
